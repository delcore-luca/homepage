Tutorials for the BSc course Mathematics (1D) for Applied Sciences and Engineering -- academic years 2024/25, 2025/26.

Description
======

This course develops the fundamental mathematical skills essential to many fields of applied science and engineering. Designed primarily for students in Architectural Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Design, Manufacture and Engineering Management, it also serves as an accessible elective for anyone seeking a solid introduction to university-level mathematics. Across two semesters, the course builds a coherent understanding of algebraic methods, calculus, and foundational mathematical structures. Students begin with core ideas in algebra, functions, binomial expansion, equation solving, and trigonometry before progressing to the principles of calculus, including differentiation, standard derivative forms, rules of differentiation, and both indefinite and definite integration. The course then advances to more sophisticated techniques through implicit and parametric differentiation, a range of integration methods, and practical applications such as graph sketching, determining areas between curves, and solving optimisation problems. Finally, students explore matrices, vectors, and complex numbers, developing confidence with matrix and vector algebra and gaining an understanding of the origin, definition, and arithmetic of complex numbers, including complex roots of quadratic equations. Throughout, the class emphasizes both conceptual understanding and practical problem-solving skills that support further study and professional practice in technical disciplines.
