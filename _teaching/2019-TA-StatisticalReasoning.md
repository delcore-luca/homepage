Teaching Assistant (TA) for the course Statistical Reasoning for academic years 2019-20 and  2020-21.

Description
======

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to Bayesian statistical modeling and computation. Students will learn to formulate and model statistical problems using Bayesian probabilistic frameworks, derive posterior distributions for standard models with conjugate priors—such as Bernoulli-Beta, Binomial-Beta, Poisson-Gamma, Normal-Normal, Normal-Gamma, and Normal-Normal-Gamma—and compute predictive and full conditional distributions. Students will further explore computational methods for situations where analytical solutions are not feasible, including Monte Carlo approximations and Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) algorithms, such as Metropolis-Hastings and Gibbs sampling. Advanced topics include graphical models and hierarchical Bayesian models. Hands-on computer practicals, starting with a tutorial on R programming, allow students to implement Bayesian models and computational algorithms, reinforcing both theoretical understanding and practical skills.
